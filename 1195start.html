Oral Examination Instructions

The oral test you are about to take will consist of the following two parts:

Part 1 – Reading Aloud
You will be required to read aloud a text.

Part 2 – Spoken Interaction
You will engage in a discussion with the Examiner on a topic based on a video clip.

When you click on START, you will have 10 minutes to study the text and watch the video clip.
